# beambugs

Evidence for defects in accelerator and beam-physics codes: what goes wrong, a script that shows it, and who established it.
Each entry was found and independently confirmed by sessions of at least two different AI models, the confirmation with its own method, and is reproduced by a standalone script.
See [Methodology](methodology.md) for the criteria.

This is not an issue tracker.
Where an entry has been reported upstream, its page links the issue or pull request.
